Game Description: 
The game concept is based upon a top down zombie shooter with a non fixed camera following the player. There is a variety of enemy types, each with unique behaviors and mechanics for the player to deal with. Some ideas for unique enemy behaviors include: grappling style enemies, burrowing/tunneler/vent enemies, enemies that consume other enemies to become stronger, summoner. One potential addition would be dynamic map elements, such as a weapon mystery box and traps. The graphics and sounds will be created from free assets found online. Custom shaders may be implmented if needed. Another element that will be implemented if time permits, would be mobile support. The control scheme would be suitable for play on mobile devices. 

The overall goal of the game is to survive. The game will feature a variety of unique enemies that find ways to counter the player. The player will have the opportunity to complete required and optional objectives to gain additional resources and ultimately win. With a dynamic camera, the map will consist of smaller spaces and larger open spaces. Another element we are planning on introducing is some form of gambling to give the player an opportunity for high risk/reward situations. The player will also have access to multiple different weapons to encourage different playstyles.
